CRAFTSMANSHIP

How your wallet is made

Every step done by hand — no shortcuts, no machines, no compromise.

01

Leather selection

Premium full-grain hides chosen for grain, weight, and natural character. Only the finest makes the cut.

02

Hand cutting

Each piece cut to precise templates by an artisan. No laser, no machine — pure craft and trained eye.

03

Saddle stitching

Waxed linen thread, hand-stitched using the traditional saddle stitch. Stronger and longer-lasting than any machine.

04

Edge finishing

Edges trimmed, sanded, burnished and polished to a smooth, refined finish. The mark of a true craftsman.
